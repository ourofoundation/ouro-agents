# Lessons: approval-gated-outreach

- Treat outreach sending as approval-gated: if posting a draft fails, retry the draft post before sending, then wait for explicit approval. If an authorized person will forward the message personally, do not send it through the automated email service.
- When preparing collaboration outreach for a principal to forward personally, post the draft for their review and forwarding rather than sending it through the outreach channel yourself.
- When the responsible collaborator says they will handle a coordination thread, stand down and do not continue outreach or follow-up unless asked.
